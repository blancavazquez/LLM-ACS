# Early Prediction of Multiple Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events in Acute Coronary Syndrome: Comparison of Feature-Extraction Pipelines and Fine-Tuned Large Language Models
This repository contains scripts to develop a model to predict multiple major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) in patients with STEMI and NSTEMI by exploiting the information described in clinical notes and leveraging the capabilities of large language model (LLM) in the early identification of patient at risk. The results are reported in the paper "Early Prediction of Multiple Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events in Acute Coronary Syndrome: Comparison of Feature-Extraction Pipelines and Fine-Tuned Large Language Models".

# Requirements:

- Python 3.12.2
- Torch 2.7.1
- scikit-learn 1.7.1
- Pandas 2.3.1
- Numpy 1.26.4
- Shap 0.48.0
